# Addressing Schemes

Addressing schemes define **how data is sent on a network**.

There are **3 types of addressing schemes**:

1. Unicast
2. Broadcast
3. Multicast

# 1. Unicast

**Unicast** is a one-to-one communication method where data is sent from one sender to one specific receiver.
### Example

- Your computer accessing a website
- Sending an email to one person

# 2. Broadcast

**Broadcast** is a one-to-all communication method where data is sent from one sender to all devices on the same network.
### Example

- ARP Request: "Who has 192.168.1.10?"
- Network announcements

# 3. Multicast

**Multicast** is a one-to-many communication method where data is sent only to a specific group of devices that have joined the multicast group.

### Example

- Live video streaming
- Online webinars
- Video conferencing

# Difference Between Unicast, Broadcast, and Multicast

|Feature|Unicast|Broadcast|Multicast|
|---|---|---|---|
|Communication Type|One-to-One|One-to-All|One-to-Many|
|Number of Receivers|One|All devices|Selected group|
|Network Traffic|Low|High|Moderate|
|Efficiency|High|Low|High|
|Example|Web browsing|ARP Request|Video streaming|
