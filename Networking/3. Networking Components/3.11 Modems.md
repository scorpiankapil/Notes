# Modems

A modem is a networking device used to connect computers or networks to the internet through telephone lines or cable connections.

Modulator + Demodulator = Modem

It converts:

- Digital signals from the computer into analog signals for transmission
- Analog signals back into digital signals for the receiving system

## Working of Modem

1. The computer sends digital data to the modem.
2. The modem converts the digital data into analog signals.
3. The analog signals travel through telephone or cable lines.
4. The receiving modem converts the analog signals back into digital data.
5. The receiving computer can then read and use the data.

### Difference Between Analog and Digital Signals

|Analog Signal|Digital Signal|
|---|---|
|Continuous in time and amplitude|Discrete in time and amplitude|
|Infinite possible values|Finite values (usually 0 and 1)|
|More sensitive to noise|Less sensitive to noise|
|Harder to store and process|Easier to store and process|
|Example: Microphone output|Example: Computer data|
