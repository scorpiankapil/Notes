# ISDN (Integrated Services Digital Network)

ISDN is a digital communication technology that allows voice, video, and data to be transmitted over traditional telephone lines.

It was designed to replace old analog telephone systems with faster digital communication.

- Digital communication technology
- Supports voice and data together
- Faster than traditional dial-up connections
- Uses telephone lines
- Provides reliable communication

## Working of ISDN

1. Voice, video, or data is converted into digital signals.
2. ISDN sends the digital signals through telephone lines.
3. Multiple types of communication (voice and data) can travel together at the same time.
4. The receiving system receives the digital signals.
5. The signals are processed so users can communicate clearly and quickly.
