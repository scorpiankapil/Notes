## Internet

The Internet is used when information or applications need to be available to everyone in the world. Internet applications use protocols such as HTTP, FTP, and SMTP and can be accessed from anywhere through the Internet.

Example: Online banking websites that allow users to transfer money from home.

## Intranet

An intranet is a private network used only inside a company or organization. It also uses Internet-type protocols like HTTP and FTP, but the applications are accessible only to internal employees and not to the public.

Example: A bank employee accessing customer account details through an internal web application.

## Extranet

An extranet is created when a company extends its intranet to selected business partners, customers, or external users. It is not available to the general public.

Example: A company allowing selected clients or partners to access internal business information.
