Network media are the cables or wireless methods used to connect devices in a network and transfer data between them. The three main cable types are:

- Coaxial cable
- Twisted-pair cable
- Fiber-optic cable

Data transfer speed is measured in Mbps (Megabits per second).

# Coaxial Cable

Coaxial cable contains a copper core with multiple insulation layers that protect against interference.

Two types of coaxial cable are used:

## Thinnet (RG-58)

- Flexible and thin cable
- Maximum distance: 185 meters
- Speed: 10 Mbps
- Uses BNC connector

## Thicknet (RG-8)

- Thicker cable
- Maximum distance: 500 meters
- Speed: 10 Mbps
- Uses AUI connector
- Mostly used as backbone cable

### Important Points

- Coax is resistant to interference
- RG-59 and RG-6 are used in TVs and video devices

# Twisted-Pair Cable

Twisted-pair cable contains twisted wires that reduce interference and crosstalk.

Two types:

- UTP (Unshielded Twisted Pair)
- STP (Shielded Twisted Pair)

## UTP Cable

UTP is the most commonly used network cable today.

- Maximum distance: 100 meters
- Uses RJ-45 connector
- Flexible and easy to install

### UTP Categories

- CAT 3 → 10 Mbps
- CAT 5 → 100 Mbps
- CAT 5e → 1 Gbps
- CAT 6 → 10 Gbps

## STP Cable

STP is similar to UTP but has extra shielding for better protection against interference.

- Maximum distance: 100 meters

# RJ-11 vs RJ-45

- RJ-11 → telephone connector with 4 wires
- RJ-45 → network connector with 8 wires

# Straight-Through Cable

A straight-through cable connects:

- Computer to hub/switch

The wire order is the same on both ends.

# Crossover Cable

A crossover cable connects:

- Computer to computer directly

To create a crossover cable:

- Wires 1 and 2 are swapped with wires 3 and 6 on one end.

# 568B Wiring Standard

568B is the most commonly used wiring standard for CAT 5 and CAT 5e cables.

# Rollover Cable

A rollover cable is used to connect a computer to the console port of Cisco routers and switches for configuration.

# Fiber-Optic Cable

Fiber-optic cable uses light signals instead of electrical signals to transfer data.

Advantages:

- Very fast
- Long-distance communication
- No electrical interference
- Highly secure

## Types of Fiber Cable

### Single-Mode Fiber (SMF)

- Uses one light ray
- Used for long distances

### Multimode Fiber (MMF)

- Uses multiple light rays
- Used for shorter distances

### Fiber Characteristics

- Supports 1000+ stations
- Distance: 2 km or more
- Speed: 1 Gbps or higher

## Fiber Connectors

- ST connector
- SC connector

# Connector Types

## Coax Connectors

- BNC connector
- BNC-T connector
- BNC terminator

## Twisted-Pair Connectors

- RJ-11
- RJ-45
- Barrel connector

## Other Connectors

- F-type connector → TV coax cable
- LC and MT-RJ → fiber connectors
- USB → connects devices like mouse, printer, flash drive
- FireWire (IEEE 1394) → high-speed multimedia connection
- RS-232 → serial connection standard
