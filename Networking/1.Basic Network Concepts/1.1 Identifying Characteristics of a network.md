A network is a group of connected systems or devices that allows sharing of resources and services such as files, printers, and Internet connections. Due to the low cost of networking devices like hubs and home routers, home and small office networks have become very common.

A network setup has two main parts: hardware and software. Hardware includes the physical devices used to connect systems together, while software allows computers to communicate with each other.

Network hardware mainly consists of two components. The first is the devices or entities that share resources, such as servers and workstations. The second is the communication medium that carries data between devices, which can be a cable or a wireless medium.
